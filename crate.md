Export the products of any Shopify store, and track what changed since you last looked.

Crate is a Shopify product scraper and store tracker in one. Open a Shopify store, click the Crate button in your toolbar, and Crate loads every product the store lists, or just the collection you are on. Then:

- Download a CSV in Shopify's own import format: variants, options, prices, compare-at prices, SKUs, weights and every image, ready for Products > Import to copy the products into your own Shopify store
- Or a spreadsheet for Excel and Google Sheets with one row per variant: price, compare-at price, stock status, SKU, type, vendor, tags, dates and a link to each variant
- Or the store's full product data as JSON, or just the product links
- Come back later and Crate tells you what changed: new products, removed products, price changes, restocks and sell-outs, a competitor price tracker with nothing to set up

Free: export up to 100 products from any store or collection, and see how many products changed.
Crate Pro: export whole stores of any size, and download every change product by product. Pay once through ExtensionPay (Stripe), no subscription.

For dropshippers and store owners doing product research, spying on competitors' prices and new launches, or moving a catalogue into their own store.

Private by design: Crate only runs on the tab where you click it, and only reads the product list the store itself publishes. It has no servers, no analytics and no account.

Crate is not affiliated with or endorsed by Shopify.
