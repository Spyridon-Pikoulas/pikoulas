# Crate — Retirement and support

Crate is being retired and is no longer offered on this website for new installations.

If you already have Crate installed, your current extension can continue to work and your
existing Pro rights remain intact. We are keeping its ExtensionPay purchase record, privacy
policy, help and support available. Shopify changes can affect continued compatibility.

Keep your existing installation if you want to keep using it. If you uninstall Crate or lose
your browser profile, a new Chrome Web Store installation is not available while its listing is
unpublished. To restore Pro in an existing installation, choose **Already paid?** and use the
email address you purchased with through ExtensionPay.

If Chrome shows a notice that Crate is no longer published, see
[Crate help and the Safety Check notice](/crate/help/).

For purchase help, contact [spyridwn.pd@gmail.com](mailto:spyridwn.pd@gmail.com).
A purchase can still be refunded within 14 days of it: write from the email you paid with.
