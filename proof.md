Doneshot is for a group that shares chores: flatmates, a family, friends, gym partners. Each of you ticks a chore off the only way there is, by taking a photo of it done, and the whole group sees who did what.

No account and no sign-up: a group syncs through Apple's iCloud sharing, only the people its starter invites can join, and everyone in the group sees its photos. Doneshot has no server of its own.

- Share chores with your group: flatmates, family, friends, gym partners
- A photo is the only way to tick a chore, and everyone sees it
- Take turns, or let anyone do it
- Invite only the people you choose, no sign-up
- Widgets with the latest proof
- Report a photo, block a member; whoever started the group can remove anyone

## Free and Doneshot Pro

Doneshot is free to download and use for one group of your own with up to three chores, and joining any group. Doneshot Pro unlocks unlimited chores in every group you're in, for everyone in it, as many groups of your own as you like, reminders when your chores come round, and the lock-screen widget, with a weekly subscription, a yearly one that starts with a 7-day free trial, or one payment for good.
The App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy.

## What a photo proves

A photo in Doneshot shows what the person who took it chose to photograph, at the time it was taken. Doneshot does not check that a chore was done well, or done at all, and settling who did what is up to your group.

## Support

**Restore a purchase.** In Doneshot, open Doneshot Pro and tap **Restore**. Purchases belong to your Apple
Account, so they come back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling.

**Cancel a subscription.** Open Settings, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**, choose Doneshot and tap **Cancel Subscription**, at least 24 hours before it renews. Doneshot Pro stays until the end of the period you paid for ([Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428)).

**Refunds.** Purchases are made through Apple, so Apple handles refunds:
[request one from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223).

**Your data.** Everything you enter stays on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud account when iCloud is on, which brings it to your other devices. There is nothing to sign in to, and we cannot see or recover it. Deleting Doneshot deletes its data from the iPhone; the iCloud copy is removed in Settings > your name > iCloud. What you put in a shared group, everyone in it sees.

**Reporting and blocking.** Any member can report a photo from its menu: it is hidden for them, and whoever started the group is told and can delete it or remove the member who took it. A report can also be sent to us at the support address. Any member can block another, which hides that member's photos for them.

**Anything else.** Write to [spyridwn.pd@gmail.com](mailto:spyridwn.pd@gmail.com) with your
iPhone model and iOS version; a screenshot helps.
