# RandomFill: Random Number, Name & Team Generator

RandomFill is an add-on for Google Sheets™ that fills cells with random values that stay put:
they are written as plain values, so they don't change each time the sheet recalculates.

- **Numbers**: from a range you choose, whole or with up to 6 decimals, with or without repeats.
- **Dates**: between two dates, weekdays only if you like, with or without repeats.
- **Picks from your list**: names or other entries, from cells in the sheet or typed one per line,
  with or without repeats.
- **Passwords**: 4 to 64 characters from the kinds you choose, at least one of each, made with a
  cryptographically secure random source. They are written into the sheet, so anyone who can open
  it can see them, and its version history keeps them: move them to a password manager, then
  clear the cells.
- **Shuffle rows**: the selected rows are reordered in place, each row whole, a heading row kept
  at the top.
- **Random teams**: names from one column dealt into teams, written to a new tab, one column per
  team. Your names stay as they are.
- **Preview, then write**: the preview shows the values and changes nothing. Filled cells are
  written over only when you tick **Write over filled cells**. **Edit > Undo** and **File >
  Version history** bring back what was there before.
- **Sample tab**: no data to try it on? Create sample tab adds a tab of fictional names and
  colours.

Up to 50,000 cells per fill, lists of up to 5,000 entries. Numbers, dates, picks, shuffles and
teams are pseudo-random, for everyday use, teaching and games, not for security, gambling,
lotteries or draws with legal or financial consequences.
