Search every comment on a YouTube video, not just the few YouTube shows you, and export them to CSV, Excel or JSON.

Sift is a YouTube comment finder and exporter in one. It loads all the comments and replies of a video into a panel above the comments, then lets you:

- Search them instantly by keyword: every word must match, or switch to a regular expression
- Find comments by user: filter by author, minimum likes, comments or replies only
- Show only comments with timestamps, links or questions, or comments by the creator, hearted or pinned
- Sort by most liked or most replied
- Click a timestamp inside a comment to jump the video there
- Open any comment in its thread on YouTube
- Download what you found: CSV (opens in Excel and Google Sheets), JSON or plain text

Free: search, filters and export of up to 500 comments at a time.
Sift Pro: export every match, as many as the video has. Pay once through ExtensionPay (Stripe), no subscription.

Built for creators reading their audience, researchers and marketers who need a YouTube comment downloader for analysis, and anyone looking for that one comment they saw last week.

No scraper to set up and no API key: Sift extracts the comments right in your browser and talks only to YouTube. It has no servers, no analytics and no account; it reads nothing on any other site.
