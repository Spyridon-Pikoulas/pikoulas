# Sift — Retirement and support

Sift is being retired and is no longer offered on this website for new installations. We are
focusing our extension work on browser work-session recovery.

If you already have Sift installed, your current extension can continue to work and your existing
Pro rights remain intact. We are keeping its privacy policy and support contact available. As
YouTube changes, continued compatibility is not guaranteed.

For a maintained free comment-search alternative, see
[YCS Continued](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ycs-youtube-comment-searc/mfobjniokjbcldieppimekoibpocahed).
If you need help with an existing Sift purchase, contact
[spyridwn.pd@gmail.com](mailto:spyridwn.pd@gmail.com). A purchase can still be refunded within 14 days of it:
write from the email you paid with.
