## The service

**Printing.** PaperFill lays out the form you open it in as a copy to fill in on paper, as its
answer key, or as the responses you choose, each on its own page, and shows the layout before you
print. Printing opens your browser's print dialog. The answer key shows the right answers and
points set in the form, and short-answer answers once you have chosen the form for PaperFill in
Google's file picker. Scores are the ones Forms gave; Forms gives add-ons no answer key for grid
questions. **Check the pages before you hand them out or grade with them**: they show what the
form and Forms hold, including its mistakes.

**Importing.** PaperFill reads questions you paste, or the one Google Docs™ document or Google
Sheets™ spreadsheet you choose, shows how it read them, and adds them **at the end of the form**,
with their right answers, points and feedback, and **turns on the form's quiz setting** if any of
them has an answer or points. It does not change or delete the questions already in the form; a
question Forms refuses part way through is taken out again rather than left half made. Forms has
no undo for this: to take imported questions out, delete them in the form. **Check the preview
and the form before you share it**: PaperFill reads the right answers from how your questions are
written, and a question written ambiguously can be read differently from what you meant.
Short-answer answer keys can only be set in a form you have chosen for PaperFill in Google's file
picker; when an import has some it could not set, PaperFill asks you to choose the form, then adds
them.

the preview. One import is one click on Add that adds at least one question, including one that
stops part way after adding some; reading a file, the preview and adding short-answer keys
