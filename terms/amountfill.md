## The service

AmountFill spells numbers and amounts of money in English words, through two formulas in the
spreadsheet (`SPELLNUMBER` and `SPELLMONEY`) and a sidebar that writes the words for a column you
select, as one formula or as plain text, in the first empty column to its right. It reads amounts
typed as text when one grouping fits them. Anything else says `Not an amount` in the cell.

**AmountFill's words are only as right as the amount and the currency it read. Read them before you
rely on them, above all on a cheque, an invoice or anything else with legal effect.** Amounts go up to
15 digits, and an amount with more decimals than its currency has is rounded. A formula's words change
when its cell does, and a formula needs AmountFill to recalculate; words written as plain text do
neither.
