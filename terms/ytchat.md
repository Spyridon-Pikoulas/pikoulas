# Crowdtime: YouTube Comments as Live Chat — Terms of Service

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Crowdtime: YouTube Comments as Live Chat is a Chrome extension made by Spyridon Pikoulas. This page sets out the
terms for using it.

## The service

Crowdtime shows a YouTube video's comments as a chat beside the video, timed to the moments
they mention. It reads comments from YouTube as the page does; when YouTube changes its pages or
limits requests, the chat may be incomplete or briefly unavailable until an update. On very large
videos it reads the top comments only. Crowdtime is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.

## Price

Crowdtime is free, with every feature on every video. There is nothing to buy.

## Your part

Use Crowdtime lawfully and within YouTube's and the Chrome Web Store's terms. Comments are
written by YouTube's users and are theirs; Crowdtime only shows them.
