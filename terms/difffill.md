## The service

DiffFill compares two explicitly chosen ranges in the current spreadsheet: two tab ranges,
matched by the unique key columns and fields you map, or two single columns compared as lists of
values. Preview reads displayed values; confirmation creates a new report tab
containing counts, field differences, source row references and metadata. Existing source cells
and report tabs are not overwritten. Report tabs contain copied data and follow the spreadsheet's
existing sharing permissions. DiffFill does not decide which list is correct or merge changes
back. Reports are snapshots, not automatically synchronized views. **Create sample tabs** adds
two new tabs of fictional rows, chosen as the old and new lists, to try a comparison.

Comparison is exact displayed text: case, spaces, number/date display and leading zeroes still
present in the source matter. Equal underlying values with different display formats can differ.
Formula definitions, formatting, notes and validation rules are not audited. There is no fuzzy
matching or automatic numeric conversion. For two lists you may choose to ignore case and
surrounding spaces; nothing else is normalized. Blank/whitespace-only or duplicate keys,
invalid mappings, changed sources and oversized jobs refuse the whole comparison.

Each source supports 1–5,000 data rows, up to 21 columns and 105,021 cells including an optional
heading row. You can compare up to 20 mapped fields. Each displayed cell is at most 20,000
characters; combined source text is at most 2,000,000 characters. A report supports up to 2,000
detail lines and 4,000,000 text characters. Oversized reports are refused, not silently truncated.
These bounds are not a completion-time guarantee. Google's service limits and the same technical
limits apply to free and paid use.

Two lists are one column each, of 1–5,000 data rows; blank cells are skipped and repeats counted.
A list report lists each distinct value once, up to 2,000 values.
