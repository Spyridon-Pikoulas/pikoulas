## Free and Doneshot Pro

The free version includes one group of your own with up to three chores, and joining any group. Doneshot Pro unlocks unlimited chores in every group you're in, for everyone in it, as many groups of your own as you like, reminders when your chores come round, and the lock-screen widget.

## Subscriptions

Doneshot Pro is an auto-renewable subscription, billed weekly or yearly. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. It renews automatically for the same period and price unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends, and your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before that. Manage or cancel it in Settings > your name > Subscriptions; cancelling stops the next renewal, and Doneshot Pro stays until the end of the period you paid for.

The yearly subscription can start with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, it becomes a paid subscription and the yearly price is charged. Any unused part of a free trial ends when you buy a subscription.

## One-time purchase

Doneshot Pro can also be bought with a single payment that never renews, and lasts for as long as Doneshot is offered on the App Store. It stays with your Apple Account and comes back through **Restore** on any iPhone signed in to it.

## Your data

What Doneshot keeps, and where, is in the [Doneshot privacy policy](/privacy/proof/).

## What you share in a group

What you add to a group, photos included, is seen by everyone in it. Photograph only what you have the right to share, and people only with their consent. No nudity, harassment or anything illegal: members who post it can be removed. You agree to this in the app before you first start or join a group.

Any member can report a photo from its menu: it is hidden for them, and whoever started the group is told and can delete it or remove the member who took it. A report can also be sent to us at the support address. Any member can block another, which hides that member's photos for them. Whoever started a group decides who is in it and can remove anyone; we cannot see what a group holds.

One member's Doneshot Pro covers every group they are in, for everyone in it. If they leave a group, or their Doneshot Pro ends, the group goes back to the free limits; its chores and photos are kept.

## What a photo proves

A photo in Doneshot shows what the person who took it chose to photograph, at the time it was taken. Doneshot does not check that a chore was done well, or done at all, and settling who did what is up to your group.
