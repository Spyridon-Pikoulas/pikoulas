## The service

TranslateFill translates the presentation or form you open it in with Google's translation
service, keeping the text's formatting, and in forms the answer keys, points and pictures. You can
replace the text, add a translated copy after each slide, or keep a form's original beside the
translation. Translations are made by machine and can be wrong: check them before you use them,
above all answer keys and anything people rely on.
