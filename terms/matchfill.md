## The service

MatchFill matches one list in the spreadsheet you open it in against another: for each row of
the first list it finds the row of the second whose text in the chosen column is the most
similar, scored from 0 to 100, and counts it as a match when the score is at least the lowest
score you choose. A list is a range of up to 5,000 rows and 26 columns.

**Matches are a judgement of similar text, not of identity.** Two different people or companies
with similar names can match, and the same one typed very differently can find no match. Check
the matches before you rely on them.

**The preview changes nothing.** A run writes the matches either to a new tab, or to new columns
it inserts beside the first list: inserting columns moves the cells to their right across, but no
cell is written over. **Create sample tabs** adds two new tabs of fictional customers and orders.
**Edit > Undo** and **File > Version history** in Sheets show and restore the spreadsheet as it
was before.
