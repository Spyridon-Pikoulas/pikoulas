## The service

ChartFill draws a Sankey, Pareto, box plot, funnel, Likert, Gantt or calendar heat map chart from
cells you select, up to 5,000 rows and 26 columns, shows it in the sidebar, and on Insert puts it
in the same tab, two columns right of those cells, as a **picture**. The preview is the picture
that is inserted.

A picture does not follow your data: **when the cells change, the chart stays as it was** until
you click Redraw in ChartFill's list of charts. Redraw reads the same cells again and puts the new
picture in place of the old one, at its place and width. A redraw needs the cells' tab, and the
chart's alt text, which holds its settings, as ChartFill wrote them.

A chart shows what ChartFill reads in the cells: a Pareto chart's 49 largest categories, with the
rest summed as "Other"; a box plot's quartiles as Sheets' QUARTILE.INC works them out, with
whiskers to the furthest values within 1.5 times the box's height and the values beyond them as
points; a Sankey chart refuses flows that go round in a circle. Rows it cannot read, such as a
missing number, are left out and the sidebar says how many. **Check a chart before you rely on it
or pass it on.**

Splitting a sheet into tabs, which SplitFill did, ends with this version. The tabs SplitFill made
stay in your spreadsheets.
