## Free and Uvrella Pro

The free version includes the UV now, today's hours and the sun protection times. Uvrella Pro unlocks your burn time, reapply reminders, the lock-screen widgets, the vitamin D window and the 10-day forecast.

## Subscriptions

Uvrella Pro is an auto-renewable subscription, billed weekly or yearly. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. It renews automatically for the same period and price unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends, and your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before that. Manage or cancel it in Settings > your name > Subscriptions; cancelling stops the next renewal, and Uvrella Pro stays until the end of the period you paid for.

The yearly subscription can start with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, it becomes a paid subscription and the yearly price is charged. Any unused part of a free trial ends when you buy a subscription.

## One-time purchase

Uvrella Pro can also be bought with a single payment that never renews, and lasts for as long as Uvrella is offered on the App Store. It stays with your Apple Account and comes back through **Restore** on any iPhone signed in to it.
