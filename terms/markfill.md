## The service

MarkFill highlights, in the document you open it in, the words and phrases on the lists you keep
in its sidebar, each list in its own colour, and lists the words found with how often each was
found and the suggestion you or MarkFill gave it. The suggestions are hints for you to follow or
not; MarkFill never replaces a word.

A run changes only highlight colours. **It first removes MarkFill's highlights** (text in one of
the colours MarkFill uses) in the tabs it runs on, then highlights the matches. Text you
highlighted in another colour is left as it is, and a match inside it is not highlighted. **If
you highlight text yourself in one of MarkFill's colours, a run or Clear removes that highlight
too.** One run highlights up to 5,000 matches. Adding the sample writes a few paragraphs into the
tab you are in, and only when that tab is empty. **File > Version history** in Docs shows the
document as it was before any run, and restores it.
