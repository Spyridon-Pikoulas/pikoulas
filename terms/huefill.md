## The service

HueFill colours the text you select in the document you open it in, as you ask. **A colouring** sets
the text colour of each letter or word in the selection, or with the highlight effect its highlight
colour, and with growing letters also its font size. Spaces and line breaks keep their look; the
selection's other formatting is left as it was. **Remove colours** sets the selection's text and
highlight colours back to the document's defaults, including colours that HueFill did not set.
**Edit > Undo** and **File > Version history** in Docs show and restore the document as it was
before. A selection can hold up to 20,000 letters at a time.
