## Free and Purlick Pro

The free version includes one counter, with the Action button, the Lock Screen control, Siri and the Home Screen widget. Purlick Pro unlocks a counter for every project, pattern repeats, the Live Activity and the Lock Screen widgets.

## Subscriptions

Purlick Pro is an auto-renewable subscription, billed yearly. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. It renews automatically for the same period and price unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends, and your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before that. Manage or cancel it in Settings > your name > Subscriptions; cancelling stops the next renewal, and Purlick Pro stays until the end of the period you paid for.

The yearly subscription can start with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, it becomes a paid subscription and the yearly price is charged. Any unused part of a free trial ends when you buy a subscription.

## One-time purchase

Purlick Pro can also be bought with a single payment that never renews, and lasts for as long as Purlick is offered on the App Store. It stays with your Apple Account and comes back through **Restore** on any iPhone signed in to it.
