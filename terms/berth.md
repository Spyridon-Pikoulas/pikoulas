# Berth: Session Backup & Recovery — Terms of Service

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Berth is a Chrome extension made by Spyridon Pikoulas. This page explains its free plan,
Pro purchase, purchase restoration and refunds.

## The service

Berth saves browser-session checkpoints, writes external JSON backups when enabled, and restores
selected HTTP and HTTPS links with their window, pinned-tab and native tab-group structure.
It does not restore unsaved forms, document edits, cookies or login sessions. Keep a surviving
external backup to recover after losing a browser profile.

## Free plan and Berth Pro

The free plan includes three named workspaces and up to seven days of rolling checkpoint
history. Berth Pro costs **US$12.99 once**, through ExtensionPay and Stripe. It provides unlimited
named workspaces and up to 90 days of rolling checkpoint history, subject to the storage bounds
and history thinning described in Berth. There is no recurring subscription charge.

Reaching the free workspace limit does not remove access to existing work, imports, exports or
restores. Restore a Pro purchase through Berth's **Restore a purchase** option, using the email
address you paid with through ExtensionPay.
