## The service

StyleFill keeps your own paragraph and character styles in your Google account's storage for the
add-on, and applies them in the document you open it in. It makes a style from the formatting of
the selected text, applies a style to the selection, and when a style changes, formats again
every place in the document where it was applied. It exports your styles as text and imports
styles exported from StyleFill.

Applying or updating a style **changes the formatting of that text**: its font, size, colors and
other settings the style has, and for a paragraph style the paragraph's alignment, spacing,
indents and outline level. StyleFill marks each place it applied a style with a named range in
the document. **Text that is copied, pasted or moved into the document does not keep that mark,
so an update does not reach it**, and neither does a new paragraph typed after a styled one.
Check the result, as you would any formatting change. **File > Version history** in Docs shows
the document as it was before, and restores it.
