## The service

SizeFill sets the width and height of images in the document you open it in, as you ask: the images
you select, or every image in the body of every tab, including images in tables and images placed
with wrapped text. Images in headers and footers are left as they are. The other side follows, so each
image keeps its proportions; with "Only shrink images bigger than this", images already at or under
the size are left as they are. Only the size an image is shown at changes, not the picture.
**Edit > Undo** and **File > Version history** in Docs show and restore the document as it was before.
A run can resize up to 2,000 images, each from 2 pixels to 120 inches.
