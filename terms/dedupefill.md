## The service

Choose a range with one heading row, the columns that identify duplicates, and whether to keep
the first or last matching source row. Matching uses exact typed values: case and spaces matter,
and text, numbers and booleans remain distinct. There is no trimming, fuzzy matching or merging.
First/last means selected source-row order, not newest date or best record. DedupeFill refuses
date-valued keys, source errors and ambiguous error-looking text. Blank-key rows require explicit approval to keep each separately.
Selected hidden and filtered rows participate.

Review both partitions before explicitly creating two new tabs. The clean tab retains chosen
rows; the discarded tab preserves the other rows with numeric original/retained source-row
references. Every selected data row belongs to one output, in source order. Calculated values
and number formats are copied; formulas become results and literal text stays literal. Leading
zeroes already lost in the source are not restored. Fonts/layout, embedded links, notes,
validation, conditional formatting, filters, hidden states and protection are not copied.
Ordinary Sheets detection of plain URL text is permitted.

Source cells and existing tabs are not overwritten. Output tabs inherit existing spreadsheet
access; they are not separately private files. Later source changes do not update them. Review
the matching policy and completed pair before relying on it. Technical bounds, source-size
safeguards and Google's service limits apply to free and paid use; unsupported jobs refuse.

## Supported bounds

Select up to 5,000 data rows plus one heading row, up to 20 source columns and up to 10
key columns. The two outputs together must fit 100,000 cells, including both heading rows
and the discarded report's two added source-row reference columns. These limits apply jointly;
not every maximum-width input fits the paired-output cap. Oversized jobs refuse rather than
silently dropping rows. Additional text safeguards and Google service limits apply to free
and paid jobs.

Jobs near these limits can take several minutes; DedupeFill works in steps and shows its progress.
