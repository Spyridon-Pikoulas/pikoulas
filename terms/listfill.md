## The service

ListFill adds dependent dropdowns to the cells you select in the spreadsheet you open it in, from a
choices table you select there: each cell's choices follow the choices to its left on its row. To do
so it adds a hidden tab named "ListFill lists" of formulas and sets Google Sheets™' dropdown rules on
those cells; dropdowns put on cells that already had ListFill dropdowns replace them. Removing
dropdowns in ListFill takes their rules away and keeps the cells' values; when none are left, the tab
is deleted. With clearing on, a changed choice empties the cells to its right in the same dropdowns,
whoever edits it. One setup covers up to 2,000 rows and five levels, with up to 500 choices in one
dropdown.
