## The service

EntryFill makes a form from the header row of a table you click in or select, in the spreadsheet you
open it in, and through it adds, finds, changes and deletes that table's records. A new record goes
in the row under the last one and takes the formats, dropdown and checkbox rules and formulas of the
record above it; a change writes only the fields changed; Delete removes the record's whole row, and
leaves a row with cells filled outside the table alone. A record changed in the sheet while it is
open in EntryFill is shown again instead of being written over. One form has up to 60 fields.
