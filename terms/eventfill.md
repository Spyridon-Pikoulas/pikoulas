## The service

EventFill creates and updates Google Calendar™ events from the rows of your spreadsheets, and
imports calendar events into them. It does what the rows say, in the spreadsheet's time zone:
check the columns and the calendar you pick before you run it, and check events that matter.
Google Calendar™ limits how many changes an account can make in a short time; past it, EventFill
stops and goes on when you run it again.
