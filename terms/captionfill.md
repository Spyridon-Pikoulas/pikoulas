## The service

CaptionFill adds figure/table captions, linked references and lists in the current document,
including numbering across nested tabs. Inspect and preview changes before applying them, and
check the result before publishing or submitting your document. CaptionFill manages its own
tracked objects; it does not infer the meaning of arbitrary plain-text references, promise exact
rendered page numbers, or automatically migrate undocumented tracking from other add-ons.

An unfinished Apply can be resumed. Abandoning it keeps visible edits already made rather than
undoing them. Removing CaptionFill tracking keeps visible text and native bookmark links but ends
automatic maintenance. Google's service limits and availability apply to free and paid use.
