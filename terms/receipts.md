## Free and Recibell Pro

The free version includes three receipts or estimates, with every country's tax and the PDF. Recibell Pro unlocks unlimited receipts and estimates, your logo and signature on them, every template, and estimates turned into receipts.

## Subscriptions

Recibell Pro is an auto-renewable subscription, billed weekly or yearly. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. It renews automatically for the same period and price unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the period ends, and your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before that. Manage or cancel it in Settings > your name > Subscriptions; cancelling stops the next renewal, and Recibell Pro stays until the end of the period you paid for.

The yearly subscription can start with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, it becomes a paid subscription and the yearly price is charged. Any unused part of a free trial ends when you buy a subscription.

## One-time purchase

Recibell Pro can also be bought with a single payment that never renews, and lasts for as long as Recibell is offered on the App Store. It stays with your Apple Account and comes back through **Restore** on any iPhone signed in to it.

## Not tax advice

Recibell works out tax from the rates and settings you choose, using each country's standard rates as a starting point. Tax rules depend on where you are and what you sell, so check your documents meet them.

- A Recibell receipt is not a fiscal invoice: it is not a Mexican CFDI, a Brazilian nota fiscal or an e-invoice, and is not filed with any tax authority.
- Rates change and differ by state, region and product; set the ones that apply to you.
- Ask an accountant or your tax authority if you are unsure what a document must show.
