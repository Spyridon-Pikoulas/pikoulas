## The service

ScoreFill lets you write a short piece of music on a staff with the mouse or the keyboard, and puts it
in the document or presentation you opened it in as a picture. The picture's alt text describes the
music and holds its notes, which is how ScoreFill opens it again.

With a picture of ours selected, **Replace puts a new picture in the old one's place and removes the
old one**. If the alt text is changed or removed, the picture stays but no longer opens in ScoreFill.
**File > Version history** in Docs and Slides shows and restores the file as it was before. Text and
other pictures in the file are never changed.

**Read the music before you rely on it**: ScoreFill writes what you enter. It does not check it.
