# Marginshot: Screenshot & Markup — Terms of Service

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Marginshot is a Chrome extension made by Spyridon Pikoulas. This page explains its free plan,
Pro purchase, purchase restoration and refunds.

## The service

Marginshot captures web-page screenshots, provides local editing tools and exports images or PDF
files. Some pages restrict capture, and complex or very large pages may not capture completely.
Check your result before relying on or sharing it. Editable projects retain the original image;
covering an area in an export does not erase it from the saved editable project.

## Free plan and Marginshot Pro

The free plan includes unlimited original, unedited captures and ten new edited captures per
local calendar month. A capture uses an allowance only on its first successful edited export
or copy. Failed attempts, retries, additional formats and re-editing or re-exporting an already
counted capture do not use another allowance. Reaching the limit does not remove your saved work.

Marginshot Pro costs **US$7.99 once**, through ExtensionPay and Stripe. It removes the monthly edited
capture allowance. There is no recurring subscription charge. Restore a Pro purchase through
Marginshot's **Restore a purchase** option, using the email address you paid with through ExtensionPay.

## Refunds

A Pro purchase can be refunded within 14 days. Write to
[spyridwn.pd@gmail.com](mailto:spyridwn.pd@gmail.com) from the email address you paid with.

## Your data

Screenshots, editable projects and exported files stay under your control. Purchase verification
uses ExtensionPay and Stripe. See the [Marginshot privacy policy](/privacy/marginshot/) for local storage,
clipboard and file exports, local allowance counts and payment information.

## Your part

Use Marginshot lawfully and within the Chrome Web Store's terms. Don't try to get around the free
plan's limit or the purchase check.
Capture, keep and share only content you have the right to, and take care with personal or
confidential information in screenshots.
