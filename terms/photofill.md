## The service

PhotoFill puts photos you select from Google Photos™, Drive images you select, or images from your
computer onto new slides of the presentation you open it in. Google Slides™ keeps images of up to 4K
(3840 pixels on the long side); larger ones are scaled down to fit, and a photo Google refuses is
named rather than added. Google's picker lets you select photos, not whole albums. Recoverable-job
metadata sits in your own Google account's add-on storage, where we cannot read it. It is no longer
offered after seven days and is deleted the next time you open PhotoFill, when you discard it, or
when you remove the add-on's data. Local files may need to be selected again. Google service limits
still apply to paid use. Check the slides before you present them.
