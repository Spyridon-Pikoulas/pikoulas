## The service

CloudFill counts the words in the presentation, document or spreadsheet you open it in, or in what
you select there, shows them as a word cloud in the sidebar, and on Insert puts the cloud in the
same file as a **picture**: centred on the current slide, at the cursor in a document, or beside the
selected cells. The preview is the picture that is inserted. It reads up to the first 2 million
characters, or 200,000 cells, and shows up to 300 words.

A cloud shows the words as CloudFill counts them: common words such as "the" and "and" are left out
by a list for the language it detects or you choose, words are kept as written or changed to lower
or upper case as you choose, and phrases are kept together only when you list them (or, in Sheets,
when you count each cell as one entry). The biggest words are the most frequent; with **Fill the
shape**, the words are repeated smaller to fill it, so a word's size, not how often it appears in
the picture, shows its count. **Check a cloud before you rely on it or pass it on.**

A picture does not follow your text: **when the text changes, the cloud stays as it was.** Make a
new one and delete the old.

Filling cells with random numbers, dates, picks and passwords, shuffling rows and making teams,
which RandomFill did, ends with this version. The values and tabs RandomFill wrote stay in your
spreadsheets.
