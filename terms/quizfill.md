## The service

QuizFill reads questions you paste, or the one Google Docs™ document or Google Sheets™
spreadsheet you choose, shows how it read them, and adds them to the form you open it in.

When you add questions, QuizFill writes them **at the end of the form**, with their right answers,
points and feedback, and **turns on the form's quiz setting** if any of them has an answer or
points. It does not change or delete the questions already in the form; a question Forms refuses
part way through is taken out again rather than left half made. Forms has no undo for this: to
take imported questions out, delete them in the form. **Check the preview and the form before you
share it**: QuizFill reads the right answers from how your questions are written, and a question
written ambiguously can be read differently from what you meant.

Short-answer answer keys can only be set in a form you have chosen for QuizFill in Google's file
picker. When an import has short-answer keys it could not set, QuizFill asks you to choose the
form, then adds them.
