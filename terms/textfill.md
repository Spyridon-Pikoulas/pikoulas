## The service

TextFill changes the text of the document you open it in, as you ask: it finds and replaces in
it, cleans it, changes its case, or sorts its paragraphs, list items or table rows. It works on the
text you select or, with nothing selected, on the whole tab you are in.

**A clean or a case change rewrites the text it covers**: characters are replaced, paragraphs that
a clean judges broken mid-sentence are joined, and the fonts, sizes, colors and spacing you ask it
to clear are cleared. Bold, italics and links are kept. **A replace rewrites each match it showed
in its preview**, applies the formatting you choose, and splits a paragraph where you put in a
paragraph break. TextFill refuses to put a paragraph break in a paragraph holding a footnote or a
positioned image, and refuses a replace when the document's matches have changed since the
preview. **A sort moves the items it sorts**: Docs
cannot move an item, so TextFill puts a copy of it in its new place and deletes the original.
Comments, suggestions and bookmarks anchored in a moved item may not move with it. TextFill
refuses to sort items holding a footnote or a positioned image, and tables with merged cells.
**Edit > Undo** and **File > Version history** in Docs show and restore the document as it was
before.

put in a paragraph or line break, or run a list. One use is one such run that changed the
document; one that finds nothing to change, is refused, or fails before changing anything uses
none.
