## The service

OrgFill draws an org chart from a list of people, each with a name, a job title and the name of their
manager, and puts it in the spreadsheet or presentation you opened it in: on a slide as boxes and
connectors you can edit, in a sheet as a picture beside the rows.

**Insert adds a new chart. It changes nothing already in the file**: not your rows, not another chart.
A chart doesn't follow later changes to the list. **File > Version history** in Sheets and Slides shows
and restores the file as it was before.

**Read the chart before you rely on it**: OrgFill draws what the list says. Where the list is unclear
(a manager not in it, someone their own manager, a reporting loop, two people with one name) it says
so and puts the person at the top, or the reports under the first of the two.
