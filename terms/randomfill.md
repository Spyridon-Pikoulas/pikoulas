## The service

RandomFill works in the spreadsheet you open it in. It fills the cells you select with random
numbers, dates, entries picked from a list you give it, or passwords; or reorders the rows you
select; or deals the names in a column you select into teams. A fill covers up to 50,000 cells,
and a list to pick from or a column of names up to 5,000 entries.

**Random values.** Numbers, dates, picks, shuffles and teams are pseudo-random, for everyday use,
teaching and games; don't use them for security, gambling, lotteries or draws with legal or
financial consequences.

**Passwords** are made with a cryptographically secure random source. RandomFill writes them into
the cells you select, so anyone who can open the spreadsheet can see them, and the spreadsheet's
version history keeps them, even after the cells are cleared. We never see them. Move them to a
password manager, then clear the cells; you are responsible for who can open the spreadsheet.

**What is written where.** The preview changes nothing. A fill writes into the cells you selected,
and writes over filled cells only when you tick **Write over filled cells**. A shuffle reorders
the selected rows in place, each row whole with its number formats; it refuses rows with formulas
or merged cells. Teams go to a new tab; the column of names is not changed. **Create sample tab**
adds a new tab of fictional names and colours. **Edit > Undo** and **File > Version history** in
Sheets show and restore the spreadsheet as it was before.
