## The service

ColorSum counts, sums and averages cells of your spreadsheets by their fill or text color, from a
sidebar and with formulas. It uses the colors Google Sheets™ shows, including colors set by
conditional formatting. Alternating colors are not seen: those cells count by their own fill. When
a conditional-formatting rule sets colors from other cells (a custom formula or a color scale),
formulas over those colors update the next time ColorSum's formulas are refreshed. Google Sheets™
recalculates formulas on its own schedule; check totals that matter.
