## The service

ColorSum counts, sums and averages cells of your spreadsheets by their fill or text color, from a
sidebar and with formulas. It sees the colors Google Sheets™ reports to add-ons: colors from
conditional formatting are not among them. Google Sheets™ recalculates formulas on its own
schedule; check totals that matter.
