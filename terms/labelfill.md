## The service

LabelFill turns selected address rows in the current spreadsheet into a downloadable PDF for
the exact label layouts it supports. You choose the columns, address lines and starting slot,
then preview the result before generating it. The source cells are left unchanged. LabelFill
does not verify postal addresses, buy postage or promise compatibility with unlisted stock.

Check the preview and output before printing. Use the matching paper size and Actual size/100%
scale, and check alignment on plain paper before using label stock. PDF geometry cannot guarantee
every printer's physical alignment. Unsupported text or content that will not fit must be fixed
before output. Google's service limits and availability apply to free and paid use.

how many selected addresses that option leaves out.

The allowance is used when a PDF has been generated and its completion is recorded, rather than

output can recover from an interrupted reply without consuming the allowance again. A different
output replaces that retry record; **Forget retry history** removes it without restoring the free
allowance. Keep your downloaded PDF if you want to print it again.
