## The service

AppendFill appends rows from explicit ranges on distinct tabs in the current spreadsheet into
one new snapshot tab. You review source order, ranges, headings, output columns and every
mapping; exact headings distinguish case and spaces. Differently named fields can be mapped
explicitly. Missing-field blanks and excluded populated columns need explicit approval.
Hidden/filtered selected rows require inclusion acknowledgement and appear visibly in output.
Wholly blank selected data rows are refused rather than silently removed.

**Create sample tabs** adds two new tabs of fictional rows, chosen as the sources, to try a job.
When a selected range's first row holds numbers, dates or checkboxes, AppendFill says it may be
data rather than headings and offers **Add heading row above**: after your confirmation it
inserts one new row across the tab above that range (Column A, Column B, …); everything below it
moves down one row, unchanged. Neither uses a free job.

Rows and duplicates remain in chosen source/row order. A Source tab column records each row's
origin. Calculated scalar values and number/date formats are copied; formulas become results.
Literal text remains literal. Leading zeroes already lost in the source are not restored.
The new tab has one heading row. Fonts/layout, embedded source hyperlinks, notes, validation,
conditional formatting, filters, hidden states, protection and rich-text decoration are not
copied. Ordinary Sheets detection of plain URL text is permitted.

This is not a join, deduplication, live refresh, full sheet copy or cross-file/sharing service.
Source and existing tabs are not overwritten. The output inherits existing spreadsheet access;
it is not a separately private file. Review mappings and results before relying on them.
Technical bounds, source-size/allocation safeguards and Google's service limits apply to free
and paid use; oversized or unsupported jobs refuse instead of silently dropping rows.

Each job supports up to 5,000 output rows including the one heading row (4,999 data rows),
at most 20 source tabs and 20 selected columns per source, and up to 19 mapped output fields
plus Source tab. The whole output, including its heading, must fit within 100,000 cells;
these limits apply together.

Jobs near these limits can take several minutes; AppendFill works in steps and shows its progress.
