# OrgFill: Org Chart Maker for Google Sheets™

OrgFill is an add-on for Google Sheets™ and Google Slides™ that draws an org chart from a list of
people: a name, a job title and the manager's name on each row.

- **From your list**: in a spreadsheet, OrgFill reads the sheet or the cells you select, and guesses
  the columns from their headings. In a presentation, paste the rows from any spreadsheet, or try the
  example.
- **Preview first**: start from one person, show 2 to 5 levels or all, a wide or compact layout, four
  colour sets, with or without job titles. Previews are always free.
- **Editable on a slide**: a box for each person and a connector for each line, grouped. The
  connectors stay attached when you move a box. Up to 150 people on a slide.
- **A picture in a sheet**: beside your rows, with alt text for screen readers. Up to 500 people.
- **Problems listed**: a manager not in the list, someone their own manager, a reporting loop, two
  people with one name. Each is said in the sidebar, and the person is put at the top.

**Good to know.** A chart is drawn from the list as it is when you insert it, and doesn't change when
the list does: insert it again. OrgFill reaches only the file you open it in, and sends no names
anywhere.
