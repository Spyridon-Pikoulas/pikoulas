Bordertally counts the days you spend in each country and tells you how many you have left: under the Schengen 90/180 rule, and against the 183-day tax-residency limit of any country, in its own tax year.

Add trips by hand or find them in your photos' locations, see when a limit will run out if you stay, and get a reminder before it does. Everything stays on your iPhone: no account, no tracking.

- Schengen 90/180 days left, every day
- 183-day tax residency per country and tax year
- Trips found in your photo locations
- A reminder before a limit runs out
- Home screen widgets
- A PDF report of your days

## Not legal or tax advice

Bordertally counts from the trips you enter or import, so a count is only as right as the trips behind it. Border guards and tax authorities make their own count and decide, and theirs prevails. Bordertally does not cover:

- residence permits and long-stay visas, which let you stay longer in the country that issued them
- bilateral agreements between some Schengen countries and yours that allow extra days
- partial days: it counts the days you arrive and leave as full days, as the Schengen rules do, however few hours you spend
- tax treaties: when two countries both count you as resident, a treaty's tie-breaker rules decide, and many countries look at more than 183 days, such as your home, family or work

Check the rules that apply to you with the authority concerned or an adviser before you rely on a count.
