ColorSum is an add-on for Google Sheets™ that counts, sums and averages cells by their fill or text
color, from a sidebar or with formulas that stay up to date.

- **From a sidebar**: select cells and click Count: every color in them, with its number of cells
  and the sum and average of their numbers.
- **As formulas** (Pro): `=COUNTBYCOLOR(A2:A50, D1)`, `=SUMBYCOLOR(A2:A50, "#f4cccc")`,
  `=SUMBYCOLOR(A2:A50, D1, C2:C50)` to sum another column, `AVERAGEBYCOLOR`, `MAXBYCOLOR`,
  `MINBYCOLOR`, the same five by text color (`COUNTBYFONTCOLOR`…), and `=COLOROF(A2)` for a
  cell's color code. The color is a cell that has it or a hex code; the ranges are ordinary
  references you can copy down.
- **Refreshed when a color changes** (Pro): Google Sheets™ does not recalculate formulas when only
  a color changes; tick one box and ColorSum does it for you, or refresh from the menu.
- **Narrow access**: ColorSum only reaches the spreadsheet you use it in, never your other files.

Colors set by conditional formatting are not visible to add-ons, so ColorSum counts the colors
you apply yourself.
