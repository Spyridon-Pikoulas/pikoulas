Keep a recovery copy of the browser work you have open: windows, tabs, pinned tabs and named
tab groups. Berth is in preparation for Chrome Web Store review.

Berth saves checkpoints on your computer and writes portable JSON backups outside the browser
profile. Preview a checkpoint, choose the windows or tabs you need, then restore them into new
windows. Your existing work stays open.

A backup marked saved means Chrome completed the download. It does not mean a cloud service has
copied it or that a read-back check has passed. Backups run while Chrome is running; retain a file
outside the profile to recover after profile loss.

Free includes three named workspaces and seven days of checkpoint history. Berth Pro is a $29
one-time purchase for more workspaces and longer history. Existing checkpoints can always be
exported and restored. Checkout opens once the product is released.

Berth restores tab URLs and window/group structure. It cannot restore unsent forms, cookies,
login sessions or unsaved document edits. Session data stays on your computer. Optional daily
usage totals are off unless you choose to share them.
