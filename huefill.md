# HueFill: Rainbow & Gradient Text for Google Docs™

HueFill is an add-on for Google Docs™ that colours the text you select: a rainbow, a gradient of your
own colours, or your colours in turn, letter by letter or word by word.

- **Rainbow text, free**: select text and click Colour the selection. As often as you like.
- **Gradients and your own colours**: pick 2 to 5 colours, or start from Sunset, Ocean, Candy, Forest
  or Fire, and the letters run smoothly from the first to the last, or take them in turn.
- **Effects**: colour the highlight behind the letters instead of the letters, or make the letters grow
  from one size to another.
- **A preview** in the sidebar shows the look before you colour anything.
- **Remove colours** takes the text and highlight colours off the selection, free.

The colours are ordinary Docs text colours: they stay when the document is shared, printed, downloaded
as a PDF or opened without HueFill.

## Plans

- **Free**: the rainbow on the letters, unlimited, and Remove colours. Your own colours, gradients,
  highlights and growing letters: 10 colourings in total per Google account.
- **HueFill Pro**: $9 once per Google account, all of it as often as you like. No subscription or
  renewal. Refunds within 14 days. See the [terms](/terms/huefill/).

Open it from **Extensions > HueFill** in any document.
