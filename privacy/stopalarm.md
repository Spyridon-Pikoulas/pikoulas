# Nodstop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Nodstop is an iPhone app made by Spyridon Pikoulas. It works on your device: it has no account and
no servers of its own, and it collects no personal data. This policy says what it keeps and where.

## What Nodstop keeps

On your iPhone it keeps only what it needs to work:

- the stops you save, which stay on your iPhone
- your location during a trip, used on your iPhone to know how far your stop is and never stored or sent

None of it leaves your iPhone, other than in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or your computer, if you use them) and what you choose to share. Deleting Nodstop deletes its data from the iPhone.

Nodstop asks for these permissions with its reason first; you can change your answer in Settings at any time:

- **Location**, to know how far you are from your stop during a trip; without it, Nodstop cannot tell when you near your stop.
- **Alarms**, to ring like an alarm clock when you near your stop, even on silent; without it, Nodstop cannot ring through silent mode or a Focus.

## Purchases

Nodstop Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells the app only whether a
purchase is active; we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple's handling of
that data is in [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Analytics and sharing

Nodstop has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and shares no data with anyone. Apple gives us
App Store figures such as downloads and sales in aggregate, and crash reports only from people who
choose to share them with developers in their iPhone's settings; none of it identifies you.

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
