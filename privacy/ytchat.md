# Crowdtime: YouTube Comments as Live Chat — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Crowdtime: YouTube Comments as Live Chat shows a YouTube video's comments as a live chat beside it. It is made by
Spyridon Pikoulas. Contact: [spyridwn.pd@gmail.com](mailto:spyridwn.pd@gmail.com).

## Comments and videos

Crowdtime runs on YouTube's watch pages. It reads the comments of the video you are watching
from YouTube, in your own tab and the same way the page reads them, and shows them in its panel.
The comments are kept in the page's memory only while you watch and are not saved or sent
anywhere else. Crowdtime has no server and no account, does not keep a history of what you
watch, and sends nothing to us.

## Settings

Your chat settings and whether Crowdtime has asked you for a store review are kept in the
extension's storage in your browser. Removing the extension removes them.

## Translation

Translation is off unless you switch it on. It uses Chrome's built-in translation, which runs on
your computer: the comments are not sent to a translation service. Chrome may download its
language models from Google the first time you translate from a language.

## Limited use and sharing

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. Comments and video IDs are used only to show
the chat. Crowdtime is free and has no payment provider. Data is not used for
creditworthiness or lending. We follow the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its
Limited Use requirements.
