# Doneshot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Doneshot is an iPhone app made by Spyridon Pikoulas. It works on your device: it has no account and
no servers of its own, and it collects no personal data. This policy says what it keeps and where.

## What Doneshot keeps

On your iPhone it keeps only what it needs to work:

- your groups' chores, the photos taken to log them and who took them
- the name you give yourself in each group
- the photos you report and the members you block, on that iPhone only, so they stay hidden for you

If iCloud is on for your Apple Account, Doneshot also keeps this in your private iCloud database, so it appears on your other devices signed in to the same account. Apple stores it, encrypted, and we cannot read it; you can delete it in Settings > your name > iCloud. Deleting Doneshot deletes its data from the iPhone.

Doneshot shares a group's chores, the photos taken to log them, who took them and the names its members give themselves with the people you invite, through Apple's iCloud sharing: a group is kept in the iCloud account of whoever started it, and everyone in it sees what is in it, photos included. Only the people its starter adds in the sharing sheet can join, and the starter can remove anyone. Leaving a group removes it from your devices; deleting one you started removes it for everyone.

Reporting a photo tells whoever started the group, through the group itself. If you also send the report to us, we receive your email, the photo and the details the mail lists, and use them only to handle the report.

Doneshot asks for these permissions with its reason first; you can change your answer in Settings at any time:

- **Camera**, to take the photo that logs a chore; without it, Doneshot cannot log a chore.
- **Notifications**, to tell you when someone in your group logs a chore, and when a chore of yours comes round; they are scheduled on your iPhone, and no server sends them.

## Purchases

Doneshot Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells the app only whether a
purchase is active; we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple's handling of
that data is in [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Analytics and sharing

Doneshot has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and shares no data with anyone beyond what is said above. Apple gives us
App Store figures such as downloads and sales in aggregate, and crash reports only from people who
choose to share them with developers in their iPhone's settings; none of it identifies you.
