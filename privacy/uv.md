# Uvrella — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Uvrella is an iPhone app made by Spyridon Pikoulas. It works on your device: it has no account and
no servers of its own, and it collects no personal data. This policy says what it keeps and where.

## What Uvrella keeps

On your iPhone it keeps only what it needs to work:

- your skin type and SPF, which stay on your iPhone and never go with your location
- when you last put sunscreen on, for the reapply reminder

None of it leaves your iPhone, other than in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or your computer, if you use them) and what you choose to share. Deleting Uvrella deletes its data from the iPhone.

To get the UV forecast for where you are from WeatherKit, Apple's weather service, Uvrella sends your location to Apple, which uses it only to answer and does not link it to you; see [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Uvrella asks for these permissions with its reason first; you can change your answer in Settings at any time:

- **Location**, to show the UV where you are; without it, Uvrella has no UV to show.
- **Notifications**, to remind you when to reapply sunscreen; they are scheduled on your iPhone, and no server sends them.

## Purchases

Uvrella Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells the app only whether a
purchase is active; we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple's handling of
that data is in [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Analytics and sharing

Uvrella has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and shares no data with anyone beyond what is said above. Apple gives us
App Store figures such as downloads and sales in aggregate, and crash reports only from people who
choose to share them with developers in their iPhone's settings; none of it identifies you.
