# Sift: YouTube Comment Search, Finder & Exporter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Sift is a browser extension that loads, searches and exports the comments of the YouTube video you
are watching. This policy says what it does with data.

## What Sift handles

- **Comments of the video you open.** Sift loads the video's public comments from YouTube, the
  same way the YouTube page does. They stay in your browser tab and are gone when you leave the
  video. They are saved only to a file you export yourself.
- **Purchase status.** If you buy Sift Pro, the extension keeps a key and your purchase status in
  your browser's extension storage so that Pro stays unlocked.
- **How many times you exported.** Sift counts your exports in the same storage, only to ask
  once, after the second, whether you would review it on the Chrome Web Store. The count
  never leaves your browser.

Sift has no servers of its own. It collects no browsing history, no analytics and no personal data,
and it reads no site other than youtube.com and extensionpay.com.

## Payments

Sift Pro is sold through ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com), which uses Stripe for the payment. When
you buy, ExtensionPay collects your email address and Stripe collects your payment details. The
extension never sees your card. ExtensionPay (https://extensionpay.com) and Stripe
(https://stripe.com/privacy) are responsible for that data.

## Sharing

Sift does not sell or share data with anyone, and does not use it for anything but the features
above.
