# Trove: WooCommerce Product Export & Store Watch — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Trove is a browser extension that exports the products of the WooCommerce store open in your tab
and shows what changed since your last export. This policy says what it does with data.

## What Trove handles

- **The store's product list.** When you click Trove's toolbar button, it loads the product list
  the store publishes through WooCommerce's own public interface (`/wp-json/wc/store/`), the same
  list its shop pages are built from. It is saved only to a file you export.
- **A record of each store you export.** After loading a whole store, Trove keeps each product's
  title, address, lowest price and stock status in your browser's extension storage, on your
  computer, so that it can show what changed on your next visit. Removing the extension deletes it.
- **Purchase status.** If you buy Trove Pro, the extension keeps a key and your purchase status in
  your browser's extension storage so that Pro stays unlocked.
- **How many times you exported.** Trove counts your exports in the same storage, only to ask
  once, after the second, whether you would review it on the Chrome Web Store. The count
  never leaves your browser.

Trove has no servers of its own. It runs only on the tab where you click it, and collects no
browsing history, no analytics and no personal data.

## Payments

Trove Pro is sold through ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com), which uses Stripe for the payment. When
you buy, ExtensionPay collects your email address and Stripe collects your payment details. The
extension never sees your card. ExtensionPay (https://extensionpay.com) and Stripe
(https://stripe.com/privacy) are responsible for that data.

ExtensionPay's library uses Chrome sync storage for its purchase key, cached purchase information
and installation time when available, so Chrome may synchronize those fields across your signed-in
browsers if sync is enabled.
Store snapshots and export counts do not use sync storage.

## Sharing

Trove does not sell data. Apart from the payment services and Chrome sync described above, it
does not share data with anyone, or use it for anything but the features above.
