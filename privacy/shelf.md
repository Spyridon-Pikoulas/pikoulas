# Shelf: Playlist & Channel Exporter for YouTube — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Shelf is a browser extension that loads and exports the list of videos in the YouTube playlist or
channel you are viewing. This policy says what it does with data.

## What Shelf handles

- **The videos of the list you open.** Shelf loads the list from YouTube, the same way the YouTube
  page does. When you are signed in to YouTube, that includes your own private playlists, which
  YouTube serves to you as it does on its page. The list stays in your browser tab and is gone
  when you leave the page. It is saved only to a file you export or to your clipboard when you
  copy it.
- **Purchase status.** If you buy Shelf Pro, the extension keeps a key and your purchase status in
  your browser's extension storage so that Pro stays unlocked.
- **How many times you exported.** Shelf counts your exports in the same storage, only to ask
  once, after the second, whether you would review it on the Chrome Web Store. The count
  never leaves your browser.

Shelf has no servers of its own. It collects no browsing history, no analytics and no personal
data, and it reads no site other than youtube.com and extensionpay.com.

## Payments

Shelf Pro is sold through ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com), which uses Stripe for the payment. When
you buy, ExtensionPay collects your email address and Stripe collects your payment details. The
extension never sees your card. ExtensionPay (https://extensionpay.com) and Stripe
(https://stripe.com/privacy) are responsible for that data.

## Sharing

Shelf does not sell or share data with anyone, and does not use it for anything but the features
above.
