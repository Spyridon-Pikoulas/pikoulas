# Bordertally — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Bordertally is an iPhone app made by Spyridon Pikoulas. It works on your device: it has no account and
no servers of its own, and it collects no personal data. This policy says what it keeps and where.

## What Bordertally keeps

On your iPhone it keeps only what it needs to work:

- the trips, countries and dates you enter or import
- the limits you track
- your reminder settings

None of it leaves your iPhone, other than in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or your computer, if you use them) and what you choose to share, such as the PDF report. Deleting Bordertally deletes its data from the iPhone.

Bordertally asks for these permissions with its reason first, and works without them; you can change your answer in Settings at any time:

- **Photos**, to find the countries you were in: Bordertally reads only your photos' location and date, on your iPhone, and never the pictures themselves.
- **Notifications**, to remind you before a limit runs out; they are scheduled on your iPhone, and no server sends them.

## Purchases

Bordertally Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells the app only whether a
purchase is active; we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple's handling of
that data is in [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Analytics and sharing

Bordertally has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and shares no data with anyone. Apple gives us
App Store figures such as downloads and sales in aggregate, and crash reports only from people who
choose to share them with developers in their iPhone's settings; none of it identifies you.
