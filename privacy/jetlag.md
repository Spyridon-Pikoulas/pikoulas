# Lagmend — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Lagmend is an iPhone app made by Spyridon Pikoulas. It works on your device: it has no account and
no servers of its own, and it collects no personal data. This policy says what it keeps and where.

## What Lagmend keeps

On your iPhone it keeps only what it needs to work:

- your flights: the airports and times you enter
- your usual sleep times and plan settings

None of it leaves your iPhone, other than in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or your computer, if you use them) and what you choose to share. Deleting Lagmend deletes its data from the iPhone.

Lagmend asks for these permissions with its reason first, and works without them; you can change your answer in Settings at any time:

- **Notifications**, to remind you of each step of your plan: light, sleep, melatonin and caffeine; they are scheduled on your iPhone, and no server sends them.

## Purchases

Lagmend Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells the app only whether a
purchase is active; we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple's handling of
that data is in [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Analytics and sharing

Lagmend has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and shares no data with anyone. Apple gives us
App Store figures such as downloads and sales in aggregate, and crash reports only from people who
choose to share them with developers in their iPhone's settings; none of it identifies you.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
