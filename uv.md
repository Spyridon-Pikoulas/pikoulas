Uvrella shows the UV index hour by hour where you are, how long your skin has before it burns, and when to put sunscreen on again.

No account, no sign-up, no tracking: your skin type and settings stay on your iPhone.

- UV now and hour by hour
- Your burn time by skin type and SPF
- A reminder when it's time to reapply
- Widgets for the home and lock screen

## Free and Uvrella Pro

Uvrella is free to download and use for the UV now, today's hours and the sun protection times. Uvrella Pro unlocks your burn time, reapply reminders, the lock-screen widgets, the vitamin D window and the 10-day forecast, with a weekly subscription, a yearly one that starts with a 7-day free trial, or one payment for good.
The App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy.

## Not medical advice

Uvrella's burn times and vitamin D window are estimates from the forecast, your skin type and your SPF. Follow your sunscreen's label and a doctor's advice, especially for children.

## Support

**Restore a purchase.** In Uvrella, open Uvrella Pro and tap **Restore**. Purchases belong to your Apple
Account, so they come back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling.

**Cancel a subscription.** Open Settings, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**, choose Uvrella and tap **Cancel Subscription**, at least 24 hours before it renews. Uvrella Pro stays until the end of the period you paid for ([Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428)).

**Refunds.** Purchases are made through Apple, so Apple handles refunds:
[request one from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223).

**Your data.** Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. There is nothing to sign in to, and we cannot see or recover it. Deleting Uvrella deletes its data from the iPhone.

**Anything else.** Write to [spyridwn.pd@gmail.com](mailto:spyridwn.pd@gmail.com) with your
iPhone model and iOS version; a screenshot helps.
