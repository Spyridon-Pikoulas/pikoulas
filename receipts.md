Recibell makes a receipt or an estimate in a minute: your business details and logo, the client, the items, the tax your country charges, and a signed PDF to send from your iPhone.

No account, no sign-up, no tracking: your business, clients and documents stay on your iPhone.

- Receipts and estimates as PDFs
- Tax worked out the way your country does it
- Your logo and signature
- A client list that fills itself
- Turn an accepted estimate into a receipt

## Free and Recibell Pro

Recibell is free to download and use for three receipts or estimates, with every country's tax and the PDF. Recibell Pro unlocks unlimited receipts and estimates, your logo and signature on them, every template, and estimates turned into receipts, with a weekly subscription, a yearly one that starts with a 7-day free trial, or one payment for good.
The App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy.

## Not tax advice

Recibell works out tax from the rates and settings you choose, using each country's standard rates as a starting point. Tax rules depend on where you are and what you sell, so check your documents meet them.

- A Recibell receipt is not a fiscal invoice: it is not a Mexican CFDI, a Brazilian nota fiscal or an e-invoice, and is not filed with any tax authority.
- Rates change and differ by state, region and product; set the ones that apply to you.
- Ask an accountant or your tax authority if you are unsure what a document must show.

## Support

**Restore a purchase.** In Recibell, open Recibell Pro and tap **Restore**. Purchases belong to your Apple
Account, so they come back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling.

**Cancel a subscription.** Open Settings, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**, choose Recibell and tap **Cancel Subscription**, at least 24 hours before it renews. Recibell Pro stays until the end of the period you paid for ([Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428)).

**Refunds.** Purchases are made through Apple, so Apple handles refunds:
[request one from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223).

**Your data.** Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. There is nothing to sign in to, and we cannot see or recover it. Deleting Recibell deletes its data from the iPhone.

**Anything else.** Write to [spyridwn.pd@gmail.com](mailto:spyridwn.pd@gmail.com) with your
iPhone model and iOS version; a screenshot helps.
