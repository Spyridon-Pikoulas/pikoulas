## A preview before changes

1. Inspect the document and choose the images or tables to caption. **Show in document** helps
   identify the right object.
2. Write the caption descriptions and preview the proposed numbers, links and warnings.
3. Apply the changes. Later, preview and update again after moving or deleting a target.

Insert a linked reference at your cursor, or add a linked list of figures or tables. Missing
targets and copied tracking markers produce warnings rather than silently pointing somewhere
else. An interrupted Apply can resume when you reopen the sidebar.

## What it works with

CaptionFill supports inline images in main-body paragraphs and top-level native tables. It does
not caption floating images, drawings, equations, tables inside other tables, headers or footnotes.
It manages its own tracking; it does not import another add-on's private reference format.
Linked figure/table lists have no rendered page numbers.

Removing tracking leaves the visible text and native bookmark links in your document, but ends
automatic maintenance. Avoid concurrent document edits during Apply, and check the result before
submitting or publishing important work. Google Docs version history remains available.

## Your document stays in Google

CaptionFill accesses only the document where you use it, including its tabs. There is no AI
service or broad Drive permission. Document content and recovery data stay in Google's services;
Stripe receives your email and handles checkout, without receiving document content.
