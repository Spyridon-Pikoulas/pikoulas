## Review the combination before creating a tab

- Choose the source tabs, their order, one explicit range and one heading row per source.
  No data yet? **Create sample tabs** adds two tabs of fictional rows to try it. A range whose
  first row holds data can get a heading row added above it.
- Review output headings and each source-to-output column mapping. Matching is exact; case and
  spaces matter. You can explicitly map differently named columns rather than silently guess.
- Approve each missing-field blank and any excluded populated column. Hidden and filtered rows
  require explicit inclusion; they appear visibly in the output.
- Create one new tab, with source rows appended in the chosen order and a Source tab column.
  Duplicates are retained. Your sources and existing tabs are not overwritten.

## A snapshot, with useful values

Calculated values and number/date formats are copied; formulas become their results. Literal
text stays text, including leading zeroes already present in the source. Missing fields become
blanks. The output has one heading row; later source changes do not update the snapshot.

This is not a full sheet copy. Fonts/layout, embedded source hyperlinks, notes, validation,
conditional formatting, filters, hidden states, protection and mixed rich-text decoration are
not copied. Google Sheets™ may recognize plain URL text normally. There is no join, deduplication,
cross-file access, sharing service or email delivery. New tabs use the spreadsheet's existing
sharing permissions; they are not separate private files.

Stop at any time; a retry of the same unchanged job continues it without using another free job.

## Job limits

Each job supports up to 5,000 output rows including the one heading row (4,999 data rows),
at most 20 source tabs and 20 selected columns per source, and up to 19 mapped output fields
plus Source tab. The whole output, including its heading, must fit within 100,000 cells;
these limits apply together.

Jobs near these limits can take several minutes; AppendFill works in steps and shows its progress.

## Access and billing

email and payment information, not spreadsheet contents. The account shown in the sidebar owns
