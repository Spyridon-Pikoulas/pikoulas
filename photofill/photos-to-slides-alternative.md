# Photos to Slides alternatives: Google Photos™ into Google Slides™

Photos to Slides, by claycodes, is the most installed add-on for putting Google Photos™ on Google
Slides™: 1.77 million installs and a 2.41 out of 5 rating from 100 reviews, as of September 2026.
It is free to start, with a subscription for up to 2,000 photos and more layouts.

Its reviews from April to September 2026 are split:

- Some who paid say it was "well worth one month's subscription" and that it "handled the 300+
  photos".
- Others write "i paid for it... it just doesn't work" and "imported a total of ONE".
- Several mention a free tier of 10 photos every 6 hours, a "lack of transparency on pricing",
  and photos sized wrongly.

If it isn't working for you, these are the other ways.

## 1. No add-on: Slides' own panel, or a script

Google Slides™ can insert from Google Photos™ itself, through Insert > Image > Drive & Photos.
Each click puts one photo on the current slide.

For a whole album, you can download it from Google Photos™, put the folder in Google Drive™, and
run a short Apps Script that makes a slide per photo. Both are free, and both are written up step
by step, with the code, in
[How to add Google Photos™ to Google Slides™ in bulk](/photofill/google-photos-to-google-slides/).

## 2. Google Photos™ to Slides

This is the second add-on for it, with 231,000 installs and a 3.26 rating from 35 reviews. Bulk
import costs $7 for 3 days, and it has not been updated since June 2025. Its 2026 reviews mention
compressed images ("image quality was severely compressed") and photos in the wrong order
("imported my images out of order").

## 3. Multiple Images, YouTube to Google Slides™

This add-on is free and rated 4.59, with 658,000 installs. It works from Google Drive™ only, not
Google Photos™, so your photos have to be in Drive first.

## 4. PhotoFill (free for 30 photos a day)

I built [PhotoFill](/photofill/) around the complaints above:

- **A free tier you can use**: 30 photos a day, every day, with every layout.
- **Full quality**: each photo at its own size, up to 4K (3840 pixels on the long side). That is
  the most Google Slides™ keeps.
- **Your order**: as picked, by date taken, or by file name.
- **Pricing on the listing**: Pro has no photo limit and costs $5.99 a month or $29 a year. Since
  most slideshows are for one event, there is also a $4.99 pass for 7 days that doesn't renew.

It puts one, two or four photos a slide, with the date taken or the file name as an optional
caption. A photo it can't add is named, not dropped silently.

One limit is the same for every add-on: Google lets add-ons take only the photos you select, not a
whole album in one click. In an album, click the first photo's check mark and Shift-click the last
to select them all.
