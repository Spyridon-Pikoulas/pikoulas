# Upload multiple images to Google Slides™ from your computer

For a few pictures, Google Slides™ already has **Insert > Image > Upload from computer**. Add
and arrange the pictures yourself; you may not need an add-on. [Google's image-insertion
instructions](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/97447?hl=en) explain that free option.

If you want an ordered batch on new slides, [PhotoFill](/photofill/) lets you select local JPEG/PNG
files and check the layout before importing. I make PhotoFill. The example below uses two
synthetic PNGs, reordered and placed side by side on one new slide.

Try the [two-image starter ZIP](/media/photofill-example.zip) if you do not have pictures ready.
Unzip it and follow the README or the steps below: **Two per slide**, with 02 teal before
01 ochre. The expected new slide has 02 on the left and 01 on the right.

## Select, reorder and preview

1. Install PhotoFill from its [product page](/photofill/), open your presentation and choose
   **Extensions > PhotoFill > Open PhotoFill**. Check the Google account shown in the sidebar.
2. Under **Or select local JPEG / PNG files**, choose your images from your computer. For a small
   first try, select two files whose contents are easy to distinguish.
3. Use the earlier/later arrows beside the file names to put them in the intended order. In the
   example, move image 02 before image 01.
4. Choose **Two per slide** and check the real layout preview. For this example, use
   **Caption: None** and a white background. Selecting and previewing do not add slides.
5. Check the full selection and remaining allowance, then click the import action labelled with
   your account once. Wait for completion and inspect the new slide: the example has 02 on the
   left and 01 on the right. New slides go after the slide you were on.

Local files supply no photo date to PhotoFill. Use manual or **File name** order; date ordering
and date captions cannot recover capture dates from these uploads.

A completed saved-import message can describe a previous job while you preview a new selection.
Check the new job's completion and the actual slide, rather than treating that old message as the
result of your preview. Preview thumbnails also have less detail than the inserted images.

## Files, allowance and interrupted imports

- Use **JPEG or PNG**, up to **50 MB per local file** and **500 images per job**. Convert HEIC and
  other unsupported formats first. Local images are resized in your browser to a maximum
  3840-pixel long side, or 2560 with **Lighter file** selected.
- New users get **12 successful photo insertions free in total**, with every layout. Existing
  users retain 30/day. A **$4.99 seven-day Pro pass** is one payment with no renewal; see
  [current plans](/photofill/#plans) for subscriptions. The whole selection is checked before
  starting: choose fewer images or get Pro if it exceeds the remaining free allowance.
- If the import is interrupted or its outcome is unclear, reopen PhotoFill in the **same
  presentation** and check its saved job before retrying. Recovery is offered for seven days;
  after browser closure, select the **same original local files** again to continue. Completed
  images are preserved. If PhotoFill cannot safely identify a partial object, follow its cleanup
  message before retrying. Do not start a second import merely because a response was delayed.

Google service limits still apply to paid use. Check the finished slides before presenting.
For account trouble, use the [single-account troubleshooting guide](/google-workspace-add-on-wrong-account/).
