EventFill is an add-on for Google Sheets™ that turns the rows of a sheet into Google Calendar™
events, and a calendar's events into a sheet.

- **Sheet to Calendar**: pick the title and date columns (and, if you have them, start and end
  times, end dates, description, location, guests and color), pick one of your calendars, click
  once. A date alone makes an all-day event; a date and a time, a timed one.
- **No duplicates**: each row gets a linked Calendar event cell. Edit the sheet and run again:
  the changed rows update their events, the unchanged ones are left alone.
- **Calendar to Sheet**: any calendar you can see, between two days, into a new sheet with each
  event's times and hours, ready to edit and send back.
- **The right time**: times are read in the spreadsheet's time zone (File > Settings), across
  daylight-saving changes.
- **Narrow access**: EventFill only reaches the spreadsheet you use it in and the events of your
  calendars; it can list your calendars but not change their settings or sharing.
