# How to read YouTube comments while watching

On a computer, YouTube puts a video's comments under it, below the description. Scroll down to read
them and the video leaves the screen. The YouTube app on a phone already shows comments in a panel
under the playing video, so this is a computer problem. There are three ways around it.

## 1. Float the video with picture-in-picture

Chrome can play the video in a small window of its own while you scroll the page:

1. Right-click the video twice. The second right-click opens Chrome's own menu.
2. Choose **Picture in picture**. The video moves to a floating window you can drag and resize.
3. Scroll down to the comments. To put the video back, choose **Back to tab** in the floating
   window.

Where it falls short: the video and the comments are in two places, the floating window covers
part of the page, and the comments still come in one long list, whatever moment you are at.

## 2. Move the comments beside the video

Several free extensions move YouTube's own comments to the right of the player, where the suggested
videos usually are.

| Option | What it does | Check before relying on it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Show YouTube comments while watching](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/gonknbphdaoahjnamfjpaincammofgae) | Comments to the right of the video, suggested videos moved underneath | Its updates mention fixes after YouTube layout changes |
| [Read Comments While Watching YouTube™](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/nojdofjkkahhdklccleaaeinfklmlaga) | A split view of the video and its comments, plus volume by scrolling over the video | Open YouTube tabs need a refresh after installing |
| [Sidesy](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/mlceikceecooilkgiikkopipedhjjech) | A comments sidebar you switch on with a keyboard shortcut or a button in the comments section | Follows YouTube's dark or light mode |
| [Crowdtime](/ytchat/) | A live chat panel beside the video: comments appear at the moment they mention, new ones as they are posted | Reads a video's top 500 comment threads; videos with comments off have no chat |

Descriptions come from each publisher's store listing, checked on 27 November 2026. We have not
tested them. This guide is written by Crowdtime's developer.

## 3. Read them as a live chat with Crowdtime

The extensions above show the same list, only beside the video. Crowdtime shows the comments as a
chat that follows the video.

1. Install Crowdtime from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/crowdtime-youtube-comment/pocfkgkplocdholjidaikoodpgpincbf).
   It is free, with no account.
2. Open a video and choose **Open chat** in the Crowdtime panel beside it, or the chat button in
   the player's controls.
3. Play the video. A comment that mentions a moment, like "2:41 the drop!", appears when the video
   reaches 2:41. Other top comments drift by in between, marked with a star, and on recent videos
   new comments arrive within seconds of being posted.
4. Scroll up in the chat to read at your own pace; it stops following until you come back down.

In theatre mode and full screen the chat sits over the edge of the video, so the comments stay on
screen, and comments also fly across the picture. Both can be switched off with the gear in the
panel, as can each kind of comment.

The comments are read from YouTube in your own tab; nothing goes to a server of ours.
[Crowdtime's page](/ytchat/) has the terms and privacy policy.
