## A focused printing workflow

- Read the displayed cell values, including postcodes formatted with leading zeroes.
- Keep duplicate rows and remove empty address lines.
- Start partway through the first sheet to use remaining labels.
- Check row-specific text or overflow errors before generating a PDF.
- Download the PDF in the sidebar; no Drive permission or outside PDF service is needed.

Choose from four exact layouts: OnlineLabels OL875 on Letter (30 labels), Flexi Labels FS21
on A4 (21 labels), Avery® 5160 on Letter (30 labels), and Avery® 5163 on Letter (10 shipping
labels). LabelFill follows their supplier PDF outlines. OL875 and 5160 have different outline
positions and are separate choices. FS21's supplier names Avery® L7160 as its template reference;
no exact cross-brand alignment is promised. Print at Actual size/100% and check plain paper first.

OL875 and FS21 are product codes of their respective owners. LabelFill is independent and is not
endorsed by those suppliers. Avery is a trademark of its owner; LabelFill is not endorsed by Avery.
