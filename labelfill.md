## A focused printing workflow

- Read the displayed cell values, including postcodes formatted with leading zeroes.
- Keep duplicate rows and remove empty address lines.
- Start partway through the first sheet to use remaining labels.
- Check row-specific text or overflow errors before generating a PDF.
- Download the PDF in the sidebar; no Drive permission or outside PDF service is needed.

Choose from twelve exact layouts. Avery® on Letter: 5160 and 8160 (30 labels), 5161 (20), 5162
(14), 5163 (10 shipping labels), 22805 (24 square labels), and return labels 5167 (80) and 5195
(60). Avery® on A4: L7160 (21) and L7163 (14). Also OnlineLabels OL875 on Letter (30) and Flexi
Labels FS21 on A4 (21). LabelFill follows each supplier's template outlines. Return labels fit two
lines at 8 points. OL875 and 5160 are separate choices, and so are FS21 and Avery® L7160, whose
outlines differ slightly. No exact cross-brand alignment is promised. Print at Actual size/100%
and check plain paper first.

OL875 and FS21 are product codes of their respective owners. LabelFill is independent and is not
endorsed by those suppliers. Avery® is a trademark of its owner; LabelFill is not affiliated with or endorsed by Avery.
