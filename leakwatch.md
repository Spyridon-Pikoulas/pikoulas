LeakWatch is a Shopify app that finds your store's discount codes on coupon sites, and shuts the
leaked ones off. A code made for your email list or an influencer ends up on a coupon site, and
from then on anyone about to pay finds it and takes the discount.

- **Checks the coupon sites that list your store**: SimplyCodes, DontPayFull, WeThrift, HotDeals,
  Coupons.com, PromoCodes.com, Honey, CouponChief and DealDrop, for your own codes.
- **Replace in one click**: the discount gets a new code (the old one plus four characters) and
  keeps its value, dates and limits; the leaked code stops working. Send the new one to the
  people it was meant for.
- **Or shut it off**: a discount whose only code leaked is ended; a code that is one of several is
  deleted, and the others keep working.
- **Shared on purpose**: codes you publish yourself are left alone.
- **Usage spikes**: LeakWatch follows each code's daily use and flags a code whose use suddenly
  jumps, often the first sign of a leak on a site it doesn't check.

## Plans

Billed by Shopify.

- **Free**: coupon sites checked weekly; replace or shut off by hand.
- **Pro**: checked daily, any page you add by its address (a forum thread, a deals site), and
  automatic shut-off the moment a code is found. $9.99 a month or $99 a year, with a 7-day free
  trial. Your price stays the same for as long as you keep the plan; cancel any time from the app.

## Good to know

- Some coupon sites keep their codes behind a click or refuse automated checks; LeakWatch checks
  the ones that show codes on the page. Settings lists each page and whether it could be read.
- Coupon sites file a store under its web address or its brand name. LeakWatch starts from your
  store's primary domain; if your pages show "No page for your store", set the name in Settings.
- Codes without a digit are matched only in capitals or as you wrote them, so a code like
  `WELCOME` isn't found in a sentence that starts with "Welcome". Codes under four characters
  aren't looked for.
- Discounts with more than 50 codes are batches of one-per-customer codes and are left out.
- LeakWatch can't stop coupon browser extensions from trying codes at checkout; on Shopify, only
  Plus stores can block those.

## Try it

This page prints three sample codes so you can see LeakWatch find one on a development store:
**LEAKWATCH-TEST10**, **LEAKWATCH-TEST20** and **LEAKWATCH-TEST30**. Create a discount with one of
them, add this page's address in LeakWatch > Settings (Pro), and click Check now on the Leaks page.
