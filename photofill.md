Make a photo deck for a class, celebration, family occasion or work report. PhotoFill adds the
images you select to Google Slides™, with a layout and order you can check before importing.

**One event? $4.99 buys seven days of Pro, with no renewal.** New users can try 12 successful photo
insertions free, with every layout and full quality. Existing users keep their previous 30-photo
daily allowance.

[Install
PhotoFill](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/photofill_insert_import_photos_images_in/714992365817)
from the Google Workspace™ Marketplace, then open **Extensions > PhotoFill > Open PhotoFill**.

## Pick, preview, present

1. **Choose your source.** Select photos in Google Photos™, pick private images from Google Drive™,
   or choose JPEG/PNG files from your computer. Drive images can stay private.
2. **Check the deck before creating it.** Preview the selected photos and layout; reorder or remove
   images, choose fit or fill, and add file-name or date captions.
3. **Import with the price clear.** PhotoFill checks the full selection against your remaining free
   allowance first. Choose a smaller selection or get Pro before starting a larger job.
4. **Pause and return.** A recoverable job keeps track of completed images for seven days. Reopen it
   in the same presentation to continue. For local images, select the same original files again;
   completed slides are preserved.

Try the local workflow with the [two-image starter ZIP](/media/photofill-example.zip). Unzip it
and follow the [local-image guide](/photofill/upload-multiple-images-google-slides/) to preview
and import the sample.

PhotoFill shows the Google account used for your selected sources, allowance and billing. For a
different account, open the presentation in a window signed in only to that account.

Choose one photo per slide, two side by side or four in a grid. Fit keeps the whole photo; fill
crops the edges. Images use their own resolution up to 4K, with a lighter 2560-pixel option for every
source. New slides go after the current slide, so you can start with a title you have already made.

If an interrupted import leaves a partial object that PhotoFill cannot safely identify, recovery
asks you to remove that object before retrying, rather than risk inserting a duplicate.

## What to know before selecting

- Up to **500 images in one job**; one recoverable job per account at a time.
- Google Photos™ lets you select photos, rather than giving PhotoFill access to your whole library.
- Private Drive inputs are JPEG/PNG, at most 20 MB and 25 megapixels each. Local JPEG/PNG files can
  be up to 50 MB each and are resized in your browser before insertion.
- Convert unsupported formats such as HEIC to JPEG/PNG first, or select the photos through Google
  Photos™. Videos and unsupported files are identified before the import starts.
- Google service quotas still apply to Pro. Check the finished deck before presenting.
