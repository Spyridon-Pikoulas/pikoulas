PhotoFill is an add-on for Google Slides™ that puts the photos you pick in Google Photos™ onto new
slides, hundreds at a time.

- **Pick in Google Photos™**: Google's own picker opens in a tab; select your photos and click
  Done. Google lets add-ons take only the photos you select, not a whole album in one click: in
  an album, click the first photo's check mark and Shift-click the last to select them all.
- **Your layout**: one photo a slide, whole or filling the slide, or two or four a slide; the
  date taken or the file name as a caption; the theme's background, black or white. New slides
  go after the one you are on.
- **The order you meant**: as picked, by date taken, or by file name.
- **Full quality**: each photo at its own size, up to 4K (the most Google Slides™ keeps), or at
  most 2560 pixels across when you tick "Lighter file".
- **Big batches**: a progress line and a Stop button; a photo that can't be added is named.
- **Narrow access**: PhotoFill reaches only the photos you pick and the presentation you open it
  in, never the rest of your library or your other files.
