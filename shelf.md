Export any YouTube playlist or channel to a spreadsheet, in one click.

Open a playlist or a channel's Videos, Shorts or Live tab, press "Export list", and Shelf loads
every video in it, then:

- Downloads it as CSV (opens in Excel and Google Sheets), JSON, or a plain list of links
- Copies all the links to your clipboard
- Gives each video its position, title, link, channel, duration (and in seconds), views, age and
  video id
- Works on playlists with videos from many channels, and on a video opened from a playlist
- Follows you as you browse YouTube, light theme or dark

Free: export up to 200 videos from any list. No 30-video limit.
Shelf Pro: export whole playlists and channels, thousands of videos. Pay once through
ExtensionPay (Stripe), no subscription.

For backing up playlists, planning a course or a watch list, researching a channel's catalogue,
or moving a playlist somewhere else.

Private by design: Shelf runs in your browser and talks only to YouTube. It has no servers, no
analytics and no account, and reads nothing on any other site.
