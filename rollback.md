Rollback is a Shopify app that keeps a history of your products and collections and lets you put
any of it back yourself, from inside your Shopify admin.

- **Every change is saved**: a new version of a product or collection each time it changes,
  whether the edit came from your staff, an import, a bulk editor or another app.
- **See what changed**: each product's and collection's history shows what changed and when,
  field by field.
- **Undo just what went wrong**: undo one edit, or a single field of it, and leave the rest of the
  product as it is. Or restore a whole earlier version.
- **Bring back deleted products** with their variants, options, images, metafields and stock, on
  the sales channels they were on.
- **Put the whole catalogue back** as it was at the end of a chosen day. Manual collections come
  back in their own order.

## Good to know

- Stock levels aren't part of the history: restoring a product leaves its stock as it is now. A
  deleted product comes back with the stock it had when its last version was saved.
- Shopify deletes a product's images along with it, so Rollback keeps its own copy of each image
  and a deleted product comes back with them. Videos and 3D models can't be re-uploaded and are
  left out.
- Smart collections come back without their conditions, which you set again in Shopify.
