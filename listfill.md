# ListFill: Dependent Dropdown & Drop Down List

ListFill is an add-on for Google Sheets™ that makes dependent dropdowns: pick a country, and the next
cell offers only its states, then only that state's cities.

- **One table, up to five levels**: keep your choices as a plain table, a header row and one column
  per level (Country, State, City), one row per path. Select it, select the cells for the dropdowns,
  click Add dropdowns.
- **Every row on its own**: each row's dropdowns follow that row's choices, on up to 2,000 rows at
  once. No named ranges, no INDIRECT, no formulas to copy down.
- **Sheets' own dropdowns**: they work for everyone who edits the spreadsheet, with or without
  ListFill. Add a row to your table and it shows in the dropdowns (after 20 new choices in one
  dropdown, click Refresh all).
- **Cleared when the one before changes** (Pro): change the country, and that row's state and city
  are emptied, so no row keeps a city from another country.

ListFill keeps each dropdown cell's choices as formulas on one hidden tab named "ListFill lists" in
your spreadsheet, and nothing anywhere else. Leave that tab as it is: change or remove the dropdowns
in ListFill, and when none are left the tab goes too. Removing dropdowns keeps the cells' values.
