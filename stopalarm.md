Pick your stop and doze off: as the train or bus nears it, Nodstop rings like a real alarm, even with the phone on silent.

No account, no sign-up, no tracking: your location and your stops never leave your iPhone.

- An alarm before your stop, even on silent
- The distance left on the Lock Screen
- Saved stops for your commute
- A backup alarm at a set time

## Free and Nodstop Pro

Nodstop is free to download and use for your first three trips. Nodstop Pro unlocks unlimited trips, with one payment for good.
The App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy.

## A backup, not a guarantee

Nodstop rings from your iPhone's location, which can come late or not at all underground, in tunnels or with the battery low. For a stop that matters, set the backup alarm too.

## Support

**Restore a purchase.** In Nodstop, open Nodstop Pro and tap **Restore**. Purchases belong to your Apple
Account, so they come back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling.

**Refunds.** Purchases are made through Apple, so Apple handles refunds:
[request one from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223).

**Your data.** Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. There is nothing to sign in to, and we cannot see or recover it. Deleting Nodstop deletes its data from the iPhone.

**Anything else.** Write to [spyridwn.pd@gmail.com](mailto:spyridwn.pd@gmail.com) with your
iPhone model and iOS version; a screenshot helps.
