TextFill is an add-on for Google Docs™ that finds and replaces, cleans up pasted text, changes
its case, and sorts paragraphs, lists and table rows, in the selected text or, with nothing
selected, the whole tab.

- **Find and replace**: put in paragraph breaks, line breaks and tabs, replace with bold,
  italics, underline, a color, a highlight or a font, or only change the formatting of what is
  found. Run a whole list of pairs at once, pasted from a sheet. Match case, whole words or
  patterns. A preview shows the matches and examples before anything changes.
- **Clean pasted text**: text pasted from a PDF, an email or a web page keeps its bold, italics
  and links. Lines broken mid-sentence are joined back into paragraphs and hyphenated words
  rejoined; repeated spaces and tabs, non-breaking and invisible spaces, smart quotes and dashes
  are made plain; fonts, sizes, colors, highlights and line spacing go back to the document's
  styles. Tick the ones you want.
- **Change case**: Sentence case, Title Case, UPPERCASE, lowercase and tOGGLE cASE. Free and
  unlimited.
- **Sort**: A to Z, Z to A, or by number. The paragraphs you select, the list the cursor is in
  (nested items move with their parent and are sorted among themselves), or a table's rows by the
  column the cursor is in, keeping the header row. Formatting moves with each item.
