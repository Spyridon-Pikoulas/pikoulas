# MathFill: Equation Editor for Slides™ & Docs™

MathFill is an equation editor for Google Slides™ and Google Docs™. Write an equation in the
sidebar, insert it as a crisp image, and change it later in a click.

- **Math without learning LaTeX**: type it the way you would say it (x^2, a/b, sqrt, pi, alpha,
  <=, sum, int) and the equation builds as you type. Buttons add fractions, roots, sums,
  integrals, matrices, Greek letters and symbols. LaTeX works too.
- **A preview of exactly what goes in**, in the size and color you choose, with mistakes
  explained before anything is inserted.
- **Crisp on every slide**: drawn at four times screen resolution, in the middle of the slide or
  of the selected box, or at the cursor in a document.
- **Edit it later**: select an equation MathFill inserted, choose Edit selected equation, change it
  and Update. It keeps its place and its size.
- **Narrow access**: MathFill only reaches the file you use it in. Your equations are written and
  drawn in your browser.
