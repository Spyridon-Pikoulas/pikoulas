Purlick counts your knitting and crochet rows, or laps, stitches and people through the door, without you looking at the phone: press the Action button, tap the control on the Lock Screen, or say “Next row in Purlick” to Siri, and the count goes up.

No account, no sign-up, no tracking: your counters stay on your iPhone.

- Count with the Action button, a Lock Screen control or Siri
- Your count on the Lock Screen while you work
- Pattern repeats: row 3 of 8, repeat 5
- A counter for every project

## Free and Purlick Pro

Purlick is free to download and use for one counter, with the Action button, the Lock Screen control, Siri and the Home Screen widget. Purlick Pro unlocks a counter for every project, pattern repeats, the Live Activity and the Lock Screen widgets, with a yearly subscription that starts with a 7-day free trial, or one payment for good.
The App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy.

## Support

**Restore a purchase.** In Purlick, open Purlick Pro and tap **Restore**. Purchases belong to your Apple
Account, so they come back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling.

**Cancel a subscription.** Open Settings, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**, choose Purlick and tap **Cancel Subscription**, at least 24 hours before it renews. Purlick Pro stays until the end of the period you paid for ([Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428)).

**Refunds.** Purchases are made through Apple, so Apple handles refunds:
[request one from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223).

**Your data.** Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. There is nothing to sign in to, and we cannot see or recover it. Deleting Purlick deletes its data from the iPhone.

**Anything else.** Write to [spyridwn.pd@gmail.com](mailto:spyridwn.pd@gmail.com) with your
iPhone model and iOS version; a screenshot helps.
