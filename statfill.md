StatFill is an add-on for Google Sheets™ that writes statistics tables from your data, laid out
like the tables of the Analysis ToolPak in Microsoft Excel: the same row labels, order and
statistics, so course instructions and answer keys read the same.

- **Descriptive Statistics, Histogram, Correlation, Covariance, and Rank and Percentile.**
- **t-Tests**: paired, two-sample with equal variances, and with unequal variances; the **z-Test**
  for two means and the **F-Test** for two variances.
- **Anova**: single factor, and two-factor with and without replication.
- **Regression**, with residuals, standard residuals and the probability table, and residual, line
  fit and normal probability plots.
- **Moving Average, Exponential Smoothing, Random Number Generation, Sampling and Fourier Analysis.**
- **Checked numbers**: StatFill computes its own t, F and normal distributions. Every table is
  checked against R and high-precision reference values before each version is released; random
  numbers and random samples are checked against their distributions.
- **Your data stays as it is**: each table goes to a new sheet or an Output Range you choose, never
  over the data it analyzes, and never over filled cells without asking. StatFill only reaches the
  spreadsheet you use it in.

Where Excel's tool leaves a choice undocumented, StatFill's is this: with no Bin Range, a histogram
uses ⌈√n⌉ equal-width bins from the minimum to the maximum; give a Bin Range for Excel's exact bins.

Not in this version: the Moving Average and Exponential Smoothing charts.
