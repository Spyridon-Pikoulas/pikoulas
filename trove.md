Export the products of any WooCommerce store, every variation included, and track what changed since you last looked.

Trove is a WooCommerce product scraper and store tracker in one. Open a WooCommerce store, click the Trove button in your toolbar, and Trove loads every product the store lists, or just the category you are on, with all their variations. Then:

- Download a CSV in WooCommerce's own import format: simple and variable products, every variation with its price, sale price, SKU, stock and image, attributes, categories, tags and all images, ready for Products > Import in your own store
- Or a CSV in Shopify's import format, to copy the products into a Shopify store
- Or a spreadsheet for Excel and Google Sheets with one row per variation: price, regular price, on sale, in stock, currency, categories, brand, rating, reviews and a link to each variation
- Or the store's full product data as JSON, or just the product links
- Come back later and Trove tells you what changed: new products, removed products, price changes, restocks and sell-outs, a competitor price tracker with nothing to set up

Free: export up to 100 products from any store or category, with all their variations, and see how many products changed.
Trove Pro: export whole stores of any size, and download every change product by product. Pay once through ExtensionPay (Stripe), no subscription.

For dropshippers and store owners doing product research, watching competitors' prices and new launches, or moving a catalogue between stores.

Private by design: Trove only runs on the tab where you click it, and only reads the product list the store itself publishes. It has no servers, no analytics and no account.
