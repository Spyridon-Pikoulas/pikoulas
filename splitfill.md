## From one list to a reviewed set of tabs

- Choose one explicit rectangular range, with optional column headings, and one grouping column.

- Confirm that all selected rows are included, including hidden and filtered rows.

## Snapshots that keep values useful

Grouping uses **exact displayed text**: case and spaces matter. Blank labels form their own group.
A number displayed as `0007` and text `0007` belong to the same group, while retaining their own
value types in the output. SplitFill does not restore leading zeroes already lost in the source.

At creation, outputs copy calculated values, number/date formats and supported base cell styles,
with column widths and an optional frozen header. Formulas become their calculated values.
These are snapshots: later source changes do not update them.

Embedded source hyperlink targets, notes, validation, conditional formatting, filters, hidden
states, protection and mixed rich-text decoration are not copied. Google Sheets may detect and
style plain URL text in the output as it normally does. Hidden source rows become visible in
their output. This is not a complete sheet copy.

## Details

A job supports **1–1,000 data rows**, up to **10 columns**, **50 groups** and **10,500 output cells
including repeated headers**. An optional heading row is additional to the data-row limit. The
same limits apply to free and paid use; text-size and spreadsheet-allocation safeguards also apply.
Jobs near these limits can take several minutes; SplitFill works in steps and shows its progress.
Actual time varies with your data and Google's services; these limits are not a speed guarantee.

Merged source cells, unsupported values, formula results resembling cell errors and oversized
jobs are refused rather than silently dropping rows. Source values, displayed text and formulas
are checked each generation request. Cached source formatting/widths are checked afresh before
completion; style-only edits may therefore be detected after partial tabs have been created,
without recording completion. Changes only to omitted notes, links or rich-text annotations do
not invalidate Preview. Review the result before relying on it.

The affected output is checked before each write. All output data, grid dimensions and tab
identities/names/ownership are checked before success or a free-use debit. Edits or deletions
of earlier outputs may be detected only at final completion or reopening a completed job;
partial tabs remain preserved. Formatting/annotation edits are not independently audited or
overwritten and may remain when the checked data and grid stay exact.

Pause between requests or resume the same unchanged latest job after reopening. A recognized
retry does not use another free job. Keep checked output data unchanged until completion. See
the [terms](/terms/splitfill/) before forgetting a receipt or abandoning unfinished work; existing
tabs are preserved.

Every new tab follows the spreadsheet's **existing sharing permissions**. Splitting a class or
customer list into tabs does not make those groups private or distribute separate files.
There is no cross-file creation, automatic refresh, sharing service or email delivery.

Processing uses Google's services, with no AI, broad Drive access or outside data-processing
service. Stripe handles billing email and payment, without receiving your rows or group labels.
