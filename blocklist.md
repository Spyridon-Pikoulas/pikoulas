Stoplist is a Shopify app that refuses checkout to buyers you have blocked: the ones who filed a
chargeback, used a stolen card or keep abusing returns. They are stopped at checkout, before they
pay, instead of being cancelled after the order.

- **Block from the order**: on any order, More actions > Add to Stoplist blocks its buyer's email,
  phone and shipping address in one go.
- **Block chargebacks automatically**: turn it on, and the buyer of any order that gets a Shopify
  Payments chargeback is blocked.
- **Or add them by hand**: an email, a phone number or an address.
- **However they type it**: capitals, dots and `+` tags in Gmail addresses, phone numbers with or
  without the country code, "Street" or "St." all match.
- **Every checkout**: blocked buyers are refused on the checkout page and on express checkouts
  such as Shop Pay, with your own message.
- **The billing address is never blocked**: with a stolen card it belongs to the cardholder.
