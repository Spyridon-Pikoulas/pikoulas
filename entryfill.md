# EntryFill: Data Entry Form & Input Form

EntryFill is an add-on for Google Sheets™ that gives a table a data entry form, like Excel's Data >
Form: one record at a time, in a form beside the sheet, made from the table's header row.

- **Add rows without scrolling**: click a cell in your table and open EntryFill, and every column
  becomes a field. Type a record and press Enter: it goes under the last row, with the formats,
  dropdowns and formulas of the row above, and the form clears for the next one.
- **Fields that follow your columns**: a dropdown column becomes a list, a checkbox column a
  checkbox, a date column a date picker. Formula columns are shown, never typed over. A value a
  column's dropdown does not allow is refused before anything is written. In a Sheets table
  (Format > Convert to table), a dropdown column shows as a text field.
- **Find, edit, delete**: find any text in all fields or one and open its record, or step through
  the records. Save writes only the fields you changed. Delete takes the record's row out of the
  sheet after a second click.
- **Nothing else in your spreadsheet**: EntryFill writes only your records, with no hidden tab and no
  trigger. If someone changes a record in the sheet while you have it open, EntryFill shows their
  change instead of writing over it.

A title above the header row is passed over. To choose the table yourself, select it with its header
row first, then click Use this table. Delete leaves a row alone when it has cells filled outside the
table; delete that row in the sheet if you mean to.
